04 / BEFORE YOU LACE UPA FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

First run?
You’re in.

Do I need to be a runner?＋

No pace requirement here. Run, jog, or walk — show up with whatever energy you’ve got.

Can I come by myself?＋

Absolutely. Meeting someone new is the whole idea. Come say hi and we’ll take it from there.

What should I bring?＋

Comfortable shoes, water, and whatever you need for a few miles outside. Check the event page for any specific details.

Where do I find the latest updates?＋

Check @insyncrunning on Instagram and the linked event page for meeting details and changes.
